About

I am a PhD Research Scholar at the Norwegian School of Economics and affiliated with FAIR. My current work examines sick-leave take-up, retirement and firm adjustment, and workplace inspections.

I use administrative data and quasi-experimental research designs to study questions at the intersection of labor markets, public policy, health, and social insurance.
