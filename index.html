I’m Nadine.

PhD Research Scholar in Economics at the Norwegian School of Economics.

My research is in labor and public economics, with a particular focus on how working conditions and social-insurance systems shape individual and firm outcomes.

FAIR CELE Department of Economics, NHH

I study how institutions and workplaces shape people’s choices and outcomes.

I am a PhD Research Scholar at NHH and affiliated with FAIR and the Centre for Empirical Labor Economics. My current projects examine sick-leave take-up, retirement and firm adjustment, and workplace inspections.

My work uses administrative data and quasi-experimental research designs to study questions at the intersection of labor markets, public policy, health, and social insurance.
